Privacy Policy

Tallgrass collects no personal data.

All data — your blacklisted sites, daily time limit, and elapsed time — is stored locally on your device using chrome.storage.local. Nothing is sent over the network.

There are no analytics, no tracking, no third-party services, and no remote servers. The extension works entirely offline.

Your data never leaves your device. You can clear all stored data at any time by uninstalling the extension.
